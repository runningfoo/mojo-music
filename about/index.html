How it started

Built on a love for songs that mean something.

MOJO began with a simple idea: pair expressive vocals, versatile guitar, and an easy connection with the audience. The result is a sound that can sit beautifully in the background or pull a whole room into the moment.

From intimate acoustic sets to energetic electric performances, the duo shapes each set around the people and place in front of them.

“We don't just play songs.
We share moments.”
